### 👋 About me

I'm a final-year **Computer Engineering** student at the **Instituto Tecnológico de Costa Rica (TEC)** who likes building things that real businesses depend on every day.

- 🎯 **Technical Lead at Celeste** — I lead the team behind an online sales platform with **200+ active vendors** and a **50M+ record** database on AWS.
- 📊 I enjoy the point where **data meets business**: KPIs, dashboards, anomaly detection and reports that end in a decision.
- 🏠 I built the systems my family's businesses run on, from rentals to mortgage-backed lending and real estate.
- 🔐 Co-discovered **CVE-2026-12805**, a heap buffer overflow in DCMTK, a medical imaging library.
- 🌱 Currently learning **agentic AI**: LLM agents, tool calling and MCP.
- 💼 **Open to internship opportunities in 2027.**
